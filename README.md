# Debt Care CRM

**Debt Care CRM** is a dedicated enterprise CRM platform for debt relief, settlement management, credit consultation, and anti-harassment case tracking.

## Core Capabilities
- **Lead Qualification & Intake**: Capture, assign, and convert distressed borrower leads.
- **Customer Debt Portfolio**: Track uncollateralized loans, credit cards, banking notices, and overdue amounts.
- **Case Management**: End-to-end assistance workflows with notes and status transitions.
- **Harassment Escalations**: Track recovery agent harassment incidents and bank escalations.
- **Team & Role Management (RBAC)**: Fine-grained granular permissions for Super Admins, Operations, Case Managers, and Support staff.
